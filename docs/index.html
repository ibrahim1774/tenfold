Tenfold

Batch video editing on your iPhone. Nothing uploaded.

Tenfold takes your talking-to-camera clips and removes pauses, filler words and retakes, adds word-by-word captions, reframes for vertical, and exports up to ten finished videos at a time. Everything runs on the phone.

Support

The demo says "Sample clip missing"

This build does not include the sample clip. Import one of your own clips from Photos instead.

Captions are in the wrong language

Open the You tab, Speech, and choose the language you record in. iOS downloads the model once.

An export did not save to Photos

Check that Tenfold is allowed to add to Photos in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Photos.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tenfold. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

Open Tenfold, go to the You tab, and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
