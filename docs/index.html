Tenfold

Batch video editing on your iPhone. Your videos are never uploaded.

Tenfold takes your talking-to-camera clips and removes pauses, filler words and retakes, adds word-by-word captions, reframes for vertical, and exports a whole batch of finished videos at once. Editing and transcription run on your iPhone, and your videos, audio and captions are never uploaded. See the Privacy Policy for the usage and purchase data the app does send.

Support

Captions are in the wrong language

Open the You tab, then Speech, then Language, and choose the language you record in. iOS downloads the speech files for it once.

An export did not save to Photos

Check that Tenfold is allowed to add to Photos in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Photos.

Do I need a subscription?

Yes. Tenfold needs an active Starter, Pro or Studio subscription, or a free trial. Pro and Studio start with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers; Starter has no trial. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple and renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tenfold. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's You tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to stop the next renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone or after reinstalling

Open Tenfold and tap Restore on the subscription screen while signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with. Once you're subscribed, Restore purchases is also in the You tab.

Deleting your data

Email support@davoxa.com with the Support ID shown in the app's You tab. If you can't open the You tab or don't see a Support ID, tell us your iPhone model, your plan and roughly when you installed Tenfold. Deleting the app removes the videos and projects it stored on your iPhone. The Privacy Policy has the details.
