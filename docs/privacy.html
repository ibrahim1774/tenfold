Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Tenfold is an iPhone app that edits talking-to-camera videos. It removes pauses, filler words and retakes, adds captions and reframes your clips. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

In short: your videos, audio, transcripts and captions are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Tenfold has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. The app does send usage, device and purchase data to three services: PostHog (analytics), Superwall (paywall and subscriptions) and AppsFlyer (ad measurement). The rest of this page explains what each one receives.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Videos you import from Photos or Files, or record in the app, are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone and edited there. They are not uploaded to Tenfold or to any of the services named in this policy.
  • Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition, which runs on your iPhone. Your audio, transcripts and captions are not sent anywhere. The first time you use a language, iOS may download that language's speech files from Apple. That download comes from Apple and is covered by Apple's terms.
  • Your projects, edits, caption text, presets and settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • Finished videos are saved to your Photos library only when you export them.

How the IDs are linked

The three services' IDs are linked to each other. Superwall's user ID is sent to AppsFlyer as its customer ID and stored on your PostHog profile. Your AppsFlyer ID is sent to Superwall and stored on your PostHog profile. This lets us connect an ad, an install, a trial or subscription, and how the app is used. If you allow tracking, the AppsFlyer records also include your advertising identifier.

Deleting and reinstalling the app does not reliably give you new, unlinked IDs. AppsFlyer keeps a value in the iOS keychain so it can recognise a reinstall, and Superwall can recognise your subscription after a reinstall.

Tracking and the iOS tracking prompt

On your first tap in onboarding, iOS asks whether Tenfold may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

  • If you tap Allow, AppsFlyer and Superwall receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and use it to match your install and purchases to the ad you saw. AppsFlyer may share the results with that ad network.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared. The other data in the table is still sent, and ad results also come from Apple's privacy-preserving ad reports. The app works the same either way.

You can change your choice at any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers named above, which process it under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • PostHog: posthog.com/privacy
  • Superwall: superwall.com/privacy
  • AppsFlyer: appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Through AppsFlyer, ad networks such as Meta, TikTok and Google may receive attribution results about the ads they showed. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. These services may process data in the United States and other countries.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: only if you record a clip or a voiceover in the app. Recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • Photos: to open the videos you choose and to save your exported videos.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe your clips on your iPhone for captions and cuts.
  • Tracking: see "Tracking and the iOS tracking prompt" above.

You can change any of these at any time in the Settings app.

How long data is kept

What Tenfold stores on your iPhone stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Exported videos stay in your Photos library until you delete them there.

We keep analytics, paywall and attribution data only as long as we need it to run the app, honour your subscription and understand how the app and its ads perform. PostHog, Superwall and AppsFlyer store it under their own retention settings and policies. Apple keeps purchase records under its own policies. If you ask us to delete your data, we handle it as described below.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Ask us to access or delete your data. Email support@davoxa.com and include the Support ID shown in Tenfold, in the You tab. It is your Superwall user ID, which is also your AppsFlyer customer ID and is stored on your PostHog profile, so we can find your records with all three services. If you can't open the You tab (for example, your subscription has ended) or your version of the app doesn't show a Support ID, tell us your iPhone model, your plan and roughly when you installed Tenfold, and we will do our best to find the matching records. We will delete what we hold, or ask PostHog, Superwall and AppsFlyer to delete it. Apple's purchase records are managed by Apple.
  • Delete your videos and projects. Delete them in the app, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes the videos, projects and settings it stored on your iPhone.
  • Turn off tracking. In the Settings app, go to Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and turn Tenfold off.
  • Manage your subscription. In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Children

Tenfold is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has used Tenfold and you want their data deleted, email us.
