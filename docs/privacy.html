Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

Tenfold is an iPhone app that edits talking-to-camera videos. It removes pauses, filler words and retakes, adds captions and reframes your clips. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your videos never leave your iPhone, and Tenfold has no account system and no servers of its own.

Your videos and audio

  • Videos you import from Photos, record in the app, or add from Files are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone and processed there.
  • Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition built into iOS. Your audio is not sent to Tenfold, and it is not sent to Apple by Tenfold. iOS may download a language model from Apple for on-device use, under Apple's own terms.
  • Finished videos are saved to your Photos library only when you choose to export.
  • Deleting a project or batch in the app deletes its files from the app's storage. Deleting the app deletes everything the app stored.

What Tenfold collects

Tenfold does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or the content of your videos. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Payments and the paywall service

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Tenfold never sees your payment details. To present subscription offers and confirm your subscription, Tenfold uses Superwall, a paywall service. Superwall receives a random identifier for your device, your subscription status, and events about the paywall (for example that it was shown or that a purchase completed). Superwall's handling of that data is described in its privacy policy at superwall.com/privacy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: only if you record a clip or a voiceover inside the app. Recordings stay on your iPhone.
  • Add to Photos: to save your exported videos. Importing uses Apple's picker and needs no permission to your library.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe your clips on-device for captions and cuts.

You can change any of these at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Tenfold is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
